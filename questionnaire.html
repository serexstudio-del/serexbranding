The Brand Voice Website Questionnaire — Serex Studio

Your brand has evolved. Your website may not have caught up yet.

Most founders feel it before they can name it — the words on the homepage used to sound right, but something feels slightly off. Not wrong enough to justify a full rebrand, but present enough to notice.

This questionnaire was built for exactly that moment. Four dimensions. One honest look at whether your website is still speaking the language of the brand you have actually become.

Five minutes. Four dimensions. One honest look.

Human Voice
Does your website sound like a real person — or like a polished template someone filled in?
Question 01
When you read your website copy out loud, does it sound like you having a genuine conversation with your ideal client — or does it sound like it was written for a brochure?
Question 02
Can a stranger read your website and get a real sense of who you are as a person — your values, your perspective, your way of seeing the world?
Question 03
Does your website use language your clients actually use when they describe their own pain points — or does it default to industry terminology and professional distance?
Question 04
If you removed your logo and brand name from your website, would your copy still be recognisably yours — or could it belong to any wellness brand in your space?
Question 05
Does your website acknowledge the emotional reality of what your clients are going through before it talks about your solution?

AI Resistance
Does your copy feel genuinely crafted — or could it have been generated in seconds?
Question 06
Does your website copy feel distinctly yours — with your specific perspective, personality and imperfections intact rather than smoothed into something generic?
Question 07
Does your website avoid generic wellness phrases — like "holistic approach," "transformative journey," or "empower you to thrive" — that could appear on dozens of other brands' websites?
Question 08
Does your copy feel meaningfully different from your competitors — in structure, language and the specific way you express your point of view?
Question 09
If you read your website out loud, does it have a rhythm and cadence that feels natural — or does it sound like it was written to be read silently, not spoken?
Question 10
Do you ever re-read your website copy and feel like it's missing something — not factually, but in the way it makes you feel?

Emotional Accuracy
Does your copy meet your clients where they actually are?
Question 11
Does your website clearly articulate the specific emotional and psychological state your ideal client is in when they first encounter your brand?
Question 12
Does your copy show that you understand not just the problem your client has, but how it actually feels to have that problem?
Question 13
Does the emotional tone of your website shift appropriately for where someone is in their journey — or does it use the same energy for someone discovering you as for someone ready to buy?
Question 14
Do you include emotional detail in your copy — not just facts and features, but the texture of what it feels like to work with you or use your products?
Question 15
When someone finishes reading your website, do they feel understood — or just informed?
Section 03 — Emotional Accuracy — / 25

Clarity & Conversion
Does your website actually guide someone toward the next step?
Question 16
Does your website make it immediately clear who you are, what you do and who you're for — without requiring someone to search for it?
Question 17
Does your website have a clear and meaningful next step for every visitor — not just "contact us," but something aligned with where they are in their journey?
Question 18
Would someone who lands on your website know within 5 seconds whether they're in the right place or not?
Question 19
Does your website show the value of what you offer in a way that's concrete, not abstract — using real examples or outcomes rather than just adjectives?
Question 20
Does your website feel complete and self-contained — or does it leave you feeling like something important is missing?
